# Branching

How every choice at the board plays through the rest of the game. Story decisions come from `docs/STORY_CHANGES.md` (SC-01 to SC-03), approved by the author on 2026-09-27. This file resolves brief §11.

## The rules

1. **A pin needs a complete case.** A suspect card can be pinned only when its Motive, Opportunity and Means columns each hold at least one piece of evidence. Whoever is pinned dies that night.
2. **One complete case per board.** Every board shows two or three credible suspects, but only one of them can be completed from the evidence.
3. **Refusing.** Walking away from the board, sitting in the armchair, or waiting 60 seconds means Ward pins the complete case himself, off-screen. If the card was unfinished, he finishes it in his own handwriting.
4. **Ward's own ID.** It can go on the board as a suspect from Chapter 1 onward. A complete case against it triggers the secret ending (scene 42), including in Chapter 5, where suspect pins are refused.
