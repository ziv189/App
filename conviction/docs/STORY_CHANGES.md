## Considered and not changed

- **Trimming the early clues that point at Ward.** The bible's double bluff expects players to suspect him early: Ruth's arrest "proves" it and Pryce's death "disproves" it. The clues stay.
- **Making Night 1 more aggressive.** The monster's cornered behavior is a clue (bible: it never touches Lily). The threat will come from presentation instead: speed, sound and camera. Tracked as a Phase 4 design note, not a story change.
- **The bedroom closet.** It has a door but no interaction prompt, so Chapter 6's "nothing is wrong" morning stays clean.
