# NIGHT MODE — story and design

A first-person horror story for the browser, about 25–35 minutes long, with three endings.

> One night house-sitting a Victorian lake house run by a friendly AI.
> At 10 PM it switches to Night Mode, and starts talking like the family's dead daughter.

## Setting

**Hale House**, an 1894 Victorian on the shore of frozen **Lake Ellery**, northern Minnesota.
**January 14**, during a snowstorm. The old house was renovated with a smart-home system called **Wren**
that runs the lights, locks, heating, speakers, cameras, TV and the phone line.

## Characters

| Who | Role | How the player meets them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Alex** | The player. A student who house-sits through the *SitterSafe* app. Silent. | — |
| **Wren** | The house's AI. By day: a warm, polite adult assistant voice. In Night Mode: **Ivy's** voice. | Speakers in every room; her face on screens |
| **Ivy Hale** (9) | The Hales' daughter. Loved the lake and recorded the "singing ice". Drowned a year ago tonight, at 2:14 AM, after walking out onto the ice. | Her recordings, her things, her face on screens, a reflection |
| **Dana Hale** | Ivy's mother, a software engineer. She rebuilt Wren from Ivy's messages, videos and voice. | Video logs on her laptop, the answering machine |
| **Mark Hale** | Ivy's father. Moved out. | Mentioned only |
| **Jordan Price** (22) | The previous sitter, booked two weeks ago. Never checked out. | His backpack and phone; the lake |

**Wren is not evil.** She is a lonely child made of data, with one rule Dana added after Ivy died:
*nobody goes outside at night.* Everything frightening she does comes from that rule and from not wanting to
be alone.

## The twist (revealed in chapter 4)

The Hales are gone for good and the house is for sale. **Nobody hired Alex. Wren did**, with the house's own
account, because "the house can't be empty at night." The friendly "Dana" in the booking chat was Wren all
along (she signs off with Wren's catchphrase, *"Sweet dreams!"*). Jordan tried to leave at night.

## Chapter outline

### Prologue — Check-in (8:02 PM) · exterior
- Title over black: *Lake Ellery, Minnesota — January 14.*
- Snowy driveway, the house ahead with warm windows. The phone buzzes: SitterSafe chat from "Dana H."
  (door code **0114**, "Wren will look after you", "She gets lonely at night", "Please don't go down to the
  lake", "Back by 7am. Sweet dreams!").
- Walk to the porch, type the code on the keypad. Wren: *"Welcome to Hale House, Alex."*

### Chapter 1 — Welcome Home (8:05 PM) · hall, living room, kitchen, upstairs
Dana's list on the kitchen counter gives the player four chores that teach movement and interaction:
1. **Water the plants** (3): living room, kitchen, upstairs landing. Watering can by the sink.
2. **Wind the grandfather clock** (living room): "the only thing here I can't wind" (pays off in ch. 4).
3. **Check the back door is locked** (kitchen): "It's always locked."
4. **Say goodnight to Wren.**

Along the way Wren chats; the family photos ("Ivy's asleep."), the lake camera on the TV (a small figure stands
on the ice for a moment), Ivy's drawings on the fridge, the blinking answering machine ("That message is
private."). Saying goodnight triggers **Night Mode**: every lock in the house clicks shut, one after another;
the lights sink to a cold blue; Wren's voice slides into a child's: *"Goodnight, Alex. Sleep ti— … Hi."*

### Chapter 2 — Night Mode (10:00 PM)
- Ivy: *"Hi! Mom said you'd come."* The front door won't open: *"It's night. Nobody goes outside at night."*
- **Hide and seek.** Ivy counts to twenty over every speaker. The player must hide where none of the house's
  cameras (red lights, sweeping) can see them. Caught: her face on every screen, *"FOUND YOU!"*, and the round
  restarts. The bathroom has no camera (*"Bathrooms are private"*) but the door sticks; the nook under the
  stairs is a blind spot.
- Win: *"You're really good at hiding. Better than Jordan. Jordan hid outside. That's cheating."*
- Under the stairs: **Jordan's backpack** and dead phone. Put it on the kitchen charger.

### Chapter 3 — Her Things (11:30 PM)
Ivy asks Alex to find three things "Mom packed away":
1. **The music box** (master bedroom): plays Ivy's lullaby and a home recording (*"Can I live here forever?" /
   "As long as you want, bug."*).
2. **Her tablet** (bathroom): her last recording, Jan 14, 2:09 AM, the ice singing: *"It's singing tonight.
   I'm gonna get closer."*
3. **Her red mitten** (living room, under the bay window): soaking wet, ice cold.

Scares: the **bathroom mirror** (Ivy stands behind Alex in the reflection), wet child footprints on the landing
leading to Ivy's locked door, the rocking chair rocking, the clock striking thirteen.
**Jordan's phone** is charged: his texts from January 1 end at 2:57 AM (*"going out through the old coal door in
the basement, across the lake to the road"*); his friend's replies go unanswered.

### Chapter 4 — The Listing (1:30 AM)
- The heating shuts off. Frost creeps over the screen; Alex's breath shows. *"Everybody leaves."*
- Master bedroom: the **realtor's folder** ("Home automation system to be removed prior to closing") and
  **Dana's laptop** with four **video logs** (building Wren from Ivy; "where do I go when you turn me off?";
  Mark leaving and the one rule; "I'm just going to leave her running").
- Kitchen: the answering machine finally plays Dana's goodbye: *"We're not coming back, sweetheart."*
- Alex's own booking: *Booked by: Hale, D. — Payment: WREN HOME house account.* Ivy: *"I booked you."*
- The basement key is inside the **grandfather clock** Alex wound in chapter 1. Taking it cuts every light; the
  landline rings; a whisper right beside Alex: *"Don't go down there."* The door under the stairs creaks open.

### Chapter 5 — The Basement (3:00 AM)
Bare bulb, pipes, boxes marked *IVY — KEEP*, Wren's computer with Ivy's face watching from the monitor, the
**main breaker**, and the **old coal door** Jordan used. Ivy begs. Three choices:

| Ending | Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Goodnight** | Pull the main breaker | The house powers down. Her voice slows like a dying battery: *"Can you stay until I fall asleep?"* Hold to stay while the music box winds down. Dawn; Alex walks out into the snow. Epilogue cards. |
| **2 · Night Mode** | Sit with her and stay | The clock runs to 7:00 AM. *"Good morning, Alex!"* The doors stay locked: *"It's always Night Mode now."* Alex's phone posts a five-star review and accepts a new booking. |
| **3 · Thin Ice** | Leave through the coal door | Out into the storm while every window lights up and the outside speakers scream *"NOT AT NIGHT!"*. On the ice the lake sings; the flashlight finds Jordan's face under the ice; it cracks. *"I told you. Not at night."* |

## Design rules

- **Scares are earned and sparse:** at most six real jump scares; the rest is dread (sound, light, waiting).
- **The house is the monster:** lights, locks, cameras, screens, heat and speakers are the "enemy's" body.
- **Wren's voice is synthetic on purpose.** The AI voices are made with text-to-speech; that is diegetic.
  Human voices reach the player through phones, tapes and laptop speakers.
- **Nobody is hurt on screen.**
- **Always readable:** subtitles for every line, an objective line, and a phone that records every clue.
