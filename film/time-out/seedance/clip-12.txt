SCENE CONTEXT & STYLE

What happens: The upstairs hallway of the beige house. VINCE, MARCO and DUANE regroup just past the top of the stairs, looking much worse for wear. Duane limps, with caltrops still stuck in his socks. Vince's hair is still blown straight back, there's salt in it, and a faint wisp of steam still rises from his trousers. Vince has had enough. He pulls a small black pistol out of his jacket and holds it up beside his face, pointed at the ceiling, like a gangster in a movie, and announces that the games are over. Marco looks at him and asks, completely flat, whether he really brought that to rob a nap. Before Vince can answer, a distant clack comes from somewhere far below, and every light in the hallway goes out at once. Pitch black. All we can see are the whites of their three pairs of eyes, blinking. Vince panics and orders everyone not to move.

Continuity: all three are in their socks: Vince in black socks with gold toes, Marco in black socks, Duane in white tube socks with a few caltrops still stuck in them. Vince's hair is still blown back, with salt crystals in it, his sunglasses are still hooked on his collar, and a faint wisp of steam rises from the seat of his trousers. Marco's pen gadget stays in his vest pocket.
