SCENE CONTEXT & STYLE

What happens: This continues straight on from the moment before, in the living room of the beige house. BRAYDEN is still standing on the right end of the coffee table with a fake smile, and GLENN, his dad, is still sitting in the middle of the couch with wet, emotional eyes. Then Glenn sets his coffee down, puts on his olive ear protectors and his sleep mask with cartoon eyes printed on it, announces that he's going off duty for fifteen minutes, and tips over sideways onto the couch, stiff as a falling tree. A squeaky toy squeaks under him, and he's snoring before he finishes landing. Brayden pokes him twice. Nothing. Then Brayden slowly turns his head toward the basement door and whispers one word. His plan has started.

12 PRINCIPLES OF ANIMATION (GROUNDED VERSION FOR THIS FILM)

1. Squash and stretch: almost none. Faces and bodies keep their shape; only the cushions squash when Glenn lands.
2. Anticipation: small and natural: a bend before the wipe, a sway before the fall, eyes before the head turn.
3. Staging: one clear action per shot: the wipe, the gear, the fall, the pokes, the whisper.
4. Straight ahead and pose to pose: continuous, natural motion with no snapping into poses; in pauses, people simply hold still.
5. Follow-through: subtle. The sleeve fabric relaxes, the elastic settles, the cushions puff up and settle.
6. Slow in and slow out: Glenn's routine eases in and out; the fall starts slowly with the sway, speeds up with gravity and stops dead.
7. Arcs: Glenn falls on a clean arc pivoting at the hips; Brayden's head turn and his pokes follow smooth, short arcs.
8. Secondary action: dust in the sunbeams, the ticking clock and the steady snore, never pulling focus.
9. Timing: Glenn slow and ceremonial, the fall quick and clean, the pokes short and dry, the whisper and grin the slowest moment.
10. Exaggeration: minimal. The comedy is Glenn's sincerity and the stiff fall into instant snoring.
11. Solid drawing: consistent anatomy and volume in every frame, even mid-fall; five fingers.
12. Appeal: a sweet, broken dad and a funny, charismatic schemer. It's a comedy.
