SCENE CONTEXT & STYLE

What happens: Meanwhile, in the small, dim, wood-paneled den of the beige house. VINCE steps over the white plastic gate in the den doorway and tiptoes in, in his socks. Against the right wall sits a small black safe with a blank sheet of paper taped to its door. Vince crouches in front of it, rubs his hands together, and figures an army guy must keep gold bars in there. He pulls the handle. WHOOMP: a huge cloud of white rock salt puffs out of the safe like a giant sneeze and knocks him backward across the room. He tumbles back onto the plastic gate, knocks it flat, and ends up in a heap in the doorway, covered in salt, his hair blown straight back, a thin wisp of steam curling up from his trousers. He demands to know who keeps rock salt. Then he goes very still, as if he has heard something in the dark hallway behind him, and slowly looks over his shoulder.
