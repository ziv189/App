# TIME OUT: Scene & Shot Breakdown

50 shots · 10 scenes · **3:35 of picture + about 10s of credits = about 3:45**

## How to read each shot

- **Image** is the prompt for the **start frame**. Generate it as an image with that shot's character and set references attached.
- **Motion** is the prompt for **image-to-video**. It describes only what moves and what the camera does. Don't re-describe the look.
- **Dialogue** is the spoken lines. If you use a video model with native audio, add `CHARACTER says: "line"` to the Motion prompt. Otherwise record or generate the VO separately and lip-sync it.
- **Audio** is the music cues (see `characters.md`) and sound effects.
- **Post** is anything to add in the edit, mostly on-screen text.
- **Durations** are *edit* lengths. Generate at your model's minimum clip length (usually 5s) and trim.

**Wherever a name appears in CAPS** (GLENN, BRAYDEN, VINCE, DUANE, PETRA, OFFICER DALE), attach that character's reference image. If your model doesn't take references, paste their **look tag** from `characters.md`.

**Always add the style block** from `characters.md` to the end of every prompt.

**Never put swear words in a generation prompt.** Add them in the voice track and bleep them in the edit.
