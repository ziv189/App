# TIME OUT: Environment Prompts

There's one long prompt per location. Each one is an **empty set with no people**, described down to where every door, window and piece of furniture goes, which way the camera points, and what the light is doing. They all share the same **Style** and **Avoid** paragraphs word for word, and they match the style of `character-prompts.md`, so the sets and the cast look like the same movie.

## The house map

Every prompt follows this layout, so all the rooms agree with each other and with the shots:

- **Outside, from the street:** the front door is a little left of center under a small porch. A big picture window is to its right. Brayden's upstairs window, with frilly curtains, sits right above the picture window. The driveway with the minivan is on the far left.
- **Living room:** you walk in the front door straight into it. Facing the front wall from inside, the picture window and Glenn's little computer desk are on the left, the TV is in the middle, and the front door is on the right. The staircase runs along the wall next to the front door, so the bottom step is right by the door.
- **Facing the back of the living room:** the stairs are on the left. The basement door is tucked under the high end of the stairs. The couch sits in the middle of the back wall, with the army photo and medal case above it, then the whiteboard. An archway on the far right leads to the downstairs hallway.
- **Downstairs hallway:** runs from that archway to the kitchen at the back of the house. The den door is on the right, halfway down.
- **Kitchen:** at the back of the house. Its back door goes out to the backyard.
- **Basement:** down the stairs behind the basement door.
- **Upstairs:** the hallway runs from the top of the stairs to Brayden's room at the front of the house. The hall closet is partway down on the right, with a blank wall opposite it.

## How to use them

1. **Make the living room (couch side) first.** Put it next to your character images. If the style doesn't match, make it again with your Glenn image attached. Each prompt says to copy only the style from an attached image, never its contents.
2. **For every other set, attach that living room image for style,** with two exceptions:
   - The **living room (front door side)** gets the couch-side image, because it's the same room.
   - The **front yard** gets the Maple Court image, because it's the same house.
3. **Use the same image model and settings for all of them:** horizontal 16:9, highest quality.
4. **When you make a shot,** attach the set image along with the character images.
5. **Signs and labels come out blank on purpose.** That covers the whiteboard, the safe sign and the footlocker stencil. Add the text in your editor (list in `characters.md`).
6. **If Higgsfield cuts a prompt off,** delete the Avoid paragraph first.
