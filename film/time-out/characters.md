# TIME OUT: Cast, Sets & Style Bible

Make everything on this page **before** you make any shots. Pick one favorite image for each character and each set. Attach those as references on every shot so the characters look the same in all ~50 clips.

---

## Global style block

Paste this at the end of **every** image and video prompt:

> Stylized 3D animated feature-film look, exaggerated cartoon proportions, big expressive eyes, squash-and-stretch comedic acting, soft global illumination, warm saturated suburban colors, cinematic composition, shallow depth of field, consistent character design, no distortion, no extra fingers, no garbled text.

**Tone rule:** it *looks* like a family movie and *plays* like an adult comedy. That contrast is the joke. Don't make it dark or gritty. The brighter and cozier the house looks, the funnier the crimes against the burglars get.

## Cast

### GLENN KOWALSKI: the dad (41)

**Who he is:** He looks like the most defeated man in America: bathrobe, mismatched socks, dead eyes. He speaks in a total monotone. The twist is that he's running the whole thing. He posts the "bait" on the neighborhood app himself.

**Look tag (paste into prompts):**
> GLENN, a 41-year-old exhausted dad, soft pear-shaped dad bod, messy thinning brown hair sticking up on one side, three-day stubble, dark bags under heavy half-lidded eyes, faded maroon terry-cloth bathrobe open over a stained grey t-shirt, blue plaid pajama pants, one red sock and one green sock

**Props:** "WORLD'S OKAYEST DAD" mug · chunky black over-ear headphones · blue sleep mask printed with cartoon wide-open eyes · cheap wristwatch · beat-up laptop

**Voice:** Low, flat, gravelly monotone. He never raises his voice. Every line sounds like a hostage reading a statement.

---

### BRAYDEN KOWALSKI: the brat (4 "and three-quarters")

**Who he is:** A brat, and he doesn't hide it for a second. He's loud, grabby and sticky, and his grin is way too big with way too many teeth. He screams when he wants something and cackles when he gets it. He lies badly and doesn't care that you know. He runs the swear jar like a loan shark. The only time he goes quiet is right before something terrible happens to you.

**Reference:** [`refs/brayden-sketch.jpg`](refs/brayden-sketch.jpg) is the original drawing. Attach it as the reference image when you generate his character sheet, then use that sheet for every shot.

**Look tag:**
> BRAYDEN, a short stocky 4-year-old boy with a wide round head, big jug ears sticking straight out, short messy dark-brown hair sticking up in tufts, thick heavy eyebrows slanted down in a scheming scowl, small beady eyes that always glance sideways, a tiny upturned button nose, and an enormous ear-to-ear grin full of small jagged teeth, wearing a grubby ketchup-stained green dinosaur onesie with orange back spikes and a little tail, hood down

**Expressions for his sheet:** wicked ear-to-ear grin · screaming tantrum · scheming side-eye · cackling with his head thrown back

**Props:** red plastic toy binoculars · crayon battle plan · red RC monster truck with the SWEAR JAR duct-taped on · pink GLITTER canister with a big red button · toy teapot · green sippy cup

**Voice:** Loud, raspy and whiny. A bratty, demanding little voice with a nasty cackle. When he threatens someone he drops to a gravelly, creepy whisper.

---

### VINCE: the "mastermind" (mid-50s)

**Who he is:** A self-appointed criminal genius who talks like he's in a heist movie. He put his own name and phone number on the getaway van. He's the first to crack.

**Look tag:**
> VINCE, a short barrel-chested man in his mid-50s, slicked-over black comb-over, neat salt-and-pepper goatee, gold aviator sunglasses, chunky gold chain, black leather jacket over a tight black turtleneck, black slacks

**Props:** laminated heist plan with photos paper-clipped on · smartphone · black socks with gold toes (once his shoes come off)

**Voice:** Fast-talking, nasal big-shot swagger that cracks into a squeak when he's scared.

---

### DUANE: the muscle (30s)

**Who he is:** 6'6" and soft as a marshmallow. He's terrified of children ("they can smell fear"). He cries easily and honestly, and he's the first to say yes to the tea party.

**Look tag:**
> DUANE, an enormous 6-foot-6 gentle-giant man in his 30s, round soft face, patchy ginger beard, worried puppy-dog eyes, grey knit beanie, stretched-out navy hoodie, cargo shorts, neon-green fanny pack

**Props:** bag of gummy bears · white tube socks (they get holes after the brick hallway)

**Voice:** Deep, soft, and gentle. It wobbles when he's upset, which is always.

---

### PETRA: the brains (late 20s)

**Who she is:** Petra is the only competent member of the crew, and she knows it. Everything she says is a dry deadpan. She hates glitter more than prison.

**Look tag:**
> PETRA, a wiry woman in her late 20s, sharp black bob haircut with blunt bangs, heavy winged eyeliner, permanently unimpressed expression, black puffer vest over a black hoodie, black cargo pants, silver hoop earrings, holding a slim vape pen

**Props:** vape pen · lock picks · black socks

**Voice:** Low, dry, sarcastic, with vocal fry. She never sounds surprised, even when she's covered in glitter.

---

### OFFICER DALE: cameo (50s)

Two lines. You can cut him if you want to keep the cast to five.

**Look tag:**
> OFFICER DALE, a heavyset police officer in his 50s, huge grey walrus mustache, navy blue police uniform straining at the buttons, police cap, holding a half-eaten pink-frosted donut

**Voice:** Slow and bored. He has seen this exact thing before.

## Character sheet prompts

Make one per character. Use the same template every time:

> Character model sheet of **[LOOK TAG]**. Full-body turnaround showing front, three-quarter, side and back views, plus a row of four facial expressions: neutral, terrified, furious, smug. Plain light-grey background, even studio lighting. **[STYLE BLOCK]**

**Height lineup.** Make this one too, so the sizes stay consistent:

> Height lineup of six cartoon characters standing side by side on a plain light-grey background: BRAYDEN (tiny, knee-height), GLENN (average), VINCE (short and stocky), PETRA (slim, average height), DUANE (towering, a full head taller than everyone), OFFICER DALE (average, round). **[STYLE BLOCK]**
