# TIME OUT: Cast, Sets & Style Bible

Make everything on this page **before** you make any shots. Each character has an original sketch in [`refs/`](refs/). Upload that sketch as the reference image when you generate the character sheet. Then pick your favorite sheet for each character and attach it to every shot they're in. That keeps everyone looking the same across all ~63 clips.

---

## Global style block

Paste this at the end of **every** image and video prompt:

> Stylized 3D animated feature-film look, exaggerated cartoon proportions, big expressive faces, squash-and-stretch comedic acting, soft global illumination, warm saturated suburban colors, cinematic composition, shallow depth of field, consistent character design, no distortion, no extra fingers, no garbled text.

**Tone rule:** the house *looks* like a cozy family movie, and what happens in it is an 18+ cartoon with uncensored swearing. That contrast is the joke. The violence is slapstick, like a Looney Tunes cartoon: nobody dies, they just get hurt badly and hilariously. The brighter and cozier the house looks, the funnier the traps get.

## Cast

### GLENN KOWALSKI: the dad (41)

**Reference:** [`refs/glenn-sketch.jpg`](refs/glenn-sketch.jpg)

**Who he is:** He's ex-army and did three tours overseas. Now he's just a regular stay-at-home dad in a bathrobe. His default face is a wide-eyed, too-big grin. It's not happiness. It's the face of a man who has seen things and decided to smile through all of them, including fatherhood. He talks in a flat monotone and never panics. **He's completely blind to what a brat Brayden is.** To Glenn he's "my little angel." Snot on the bathrobe is a hug. A booby-trapped house is a good little soldier protecting his old man. If anyone calls Brayden a brat (or a psycho), Glenn is deeply offended. The twist is that he's in on it: he posts the "nap schedule" bait online himself, and Brayden learned everything from Daddy's old footlocker.

**Look tag (paste into prompts):**
> GLENN, a 41-year-old ex-army dad with thick bushy dark hair, heavy straight black eyebrows, big round wide-open eyes with a thousand-yard stare, a long straight nose, a full thick dark beard and mustache, and a big open-mouthed grin with crooked gappy teeth, broad soldier's shoulders gone soft with a dad belly, wearing a faded maroon bathrobe over an olive-green army t-shirt, metal dog tags on a chain, plaid pajama pants, one red sock and one green sock

**Props:** "WORLD'S OKAYEST DAD" mug · olive military ear defenders (his "nap headphones") · blue sleep mask printed with cartoon wide-open eyes · dog tags · cheap wristwatch · beat-up laptop · a framed photo of his younger, clean-shaven self in dress uniform with medals (on the living-room wall)

**Voice:** Low, flat, gravelly monotone. He never raises his voice. He sounds like a drill sergeant who's too tired to yell.

### BRAYDEN KOWALSKI: the brat (4 "and three-quarters")

**Reference:** [`refs/brayden-sketch.jpg`](refs/brayden-sketch.jpg)

**Who he is:** A brat, and he doesn't hide it for a second. He's loud, grabby and sticky, and his grin is way too big with way too many teeth, just like Dad's. He can't read, but he's memorized every picture in Daddy's army field manual. He screams when he wants something and cackles when he gets it. He runs the swear jar like a loan shark. He barely swears himself (he's four), and when he does it's only "bitch." The jar rule only applies to other people. The only time he goes quiet is right before something terrible happens to you.

**Look tag:**
> BRAYDEN, a short stocky 4-year-old boy with a wide round head, big jug ears sticking straight out, short messy dark-brown hair sticking up in tufts, thick heavy eyebrows slanted down in a scheming scowl, small beady eyes that always glance sideways, a tiny upturned button nose, and an enormous ear-to-ear grin full of small jagged teeth, wearing a grubby ketchup-stained green dinosaur onesie with orange back spikes and a little tail, hood down

**War-mode look tag** (from the suit-up in Scene 3 onward). Make a second character sheet for this one:
> BRAYDEN as above, plus two thick black camo-paint stripes under each eye and his dad's oversized olive army helmet, which is way too big and wobbles on his head, with his jug ears sticking out underneath

**Expressions for his sheet:** wicked ear-to-ear grin · screaming tantrum · scheming side-eye · cackling with his head thrown back

**Props:** red plastic toy binoculars · crayon battle plan · red RC monster truck with the SWEAR JAR duct-taped on top and a little grabber claw on the front · toy teapot · green sippy cup · Dad's dummy training grenade · Dad's night-vision goggles (seen only as his green POV during the blackout)

**Voice:** Loud, raspy and whiny. A bratty, demanding little voice with a nasty cackle. When he threatens someone he drops to a gravelly, creepy whisper.

### VINCE: the "mastermind" (50s)

**Reference:** [`refs/vince-sketch.jpg`](refs/vince-sketch.jpg)

**Who he is:** A self-appointed criminal genius who talks like he's in a heist movie. He painted his own name and phone number on the getaway van. He also brought a gun to rob a nap, and he ends up shooting his own guy in the leg in the dark. He's the loudest one, and he's also the first to crack.

**Look tag:**
> VINCE, a wiry man in his 50s with a big swoop of spiky jet-black hair, heavy angled black eyebrows, shifty eyes that always glance sideways, a long pointy nose, a bushy black mustache, a scruffy black goatee on a sharp pointed chin, big jug ears, crooked buck teeth, wearing a chunky gold chain, a black leather jacket over a tight black turtleneck, and black slacks

**Props:** laminated heist plan with photos paper-clipped on · smartphone · gold aviator sunglasses · a black pistol he holds sideways like a movie gangster · black socks with gold toes (once his shoes come off)

**Voice:** Fast-talking, nasal big-shot swagger that cracks into a squeak when he's scared.

### MARCO: the brains (30s)

**Reference:** [`refs/marco-and-duane-sketch.jpg`](refs/marco-and-duane-sketch.jpg) (the **left** face)

**Who he is:** The only competent member of the crew, and he knows it. He blows vape in people's faces instead of answering them. He's bored by everything and says it all in a dry deadpan. He's unimpressed even when he's pinned to a wall by twelve knives.

**Look tag:**
> MARCO, a lanky man in his 30s with messy dark hair, a long narrow face, heavy-lidded bored eyes with dark bags under them, a few moles on his cheeks and forehead, a long thin nose, a huge bushy black beard that comes to a point, and crooked jagged teeth, wearing a black puffer vest over a black hoodie and black cargo pants, holding a slim vape pen

**Props:** vape pen · lock picks · black socks

**Voice:** Low, dry and sarcastic, with vocal fry. When the knife lands between his legs, his voice jumps up an octave and stays there for the rest of the scene.

## Character sheet prompts

**Start with [`character-prompts.md`](character-prompts.md).** It has a long, full-body reference prompt for every character with matching style, framing and lighting. The turnaround template below is optional, for extra angles once those are locked.

Make one per character, uploading their sketch as the reference image. Use the same template every time:

> Character model sheet based on the reference sketch: **[LOOK TAG]**. Full-body turnaround showing front, three-quarter, side and back views, plus a row of four facial expressions: neutral, terrified, furious, smug. Plain light-grey background, even studio lighting. **[STYLE BLOCK]**

For Brayden, swap the expressions for the ones listed in his section, and make a second sheet for his war-mode look.

**Height lineup.** Make this one too, so the sizes stay consistent:

> Height lineup of six cartoon characters standing side by side on a plain light-grey background: BRAYDEN (tiny, knee-height), GLENN (average height, broad), VINCE (short and wiry), MARCO (tall and lanky), DUANE (towering, a full head taller than everyone), OFFICER DALE (average, round). **[STYLE BLOCK]**
