## CS3 The amber light: states

New asset. CS1 and CS2 don't show the amber light being pulled or letting go, and that is this scene's key moment. Without a design, the model tends to turn it into a comet.

Model: Seedream 5.0 Pro · Aspect ratio: 21:9
Attach (canon only): Image 1 CS1 (the lights), Image 2 CS2 (the little light's moods, for the layout only)

```
The amber light from the film in four states, side by side: the small glowing light that goes first, lets go of the sky and drifts down into the world. This sheet fixes how it looks when a current pulls at it and when it lets go.

From left to right, the first three at the same size:
1. Calm: exactly the amber light from Image 1, a crescent-shaped glow with its horns pointing up, an amber core painted in thick luminous brushstrokes, a dark open hollow between the horns, and a loose oval ring of short curved amber paint dabs around it.
2. Pulled: the same crescent shape, horns still pointing up, a little brighter; the lower half of its ring of dabs is drawn downward into short downward strokes, as if a gentle current below is tugging at it.
3. Letting go: the same crescent shape, horns up, at the lower end of a long soft vertical streak of amber paint; above it, a tail of small amber dabs fades upward into the dark, as if it is drifting slowly down.
4. Far away: the same light seen from very far, a tiny amber point with a short faint trail of dabs above it.
In every state the light keeps its crescent shape with the horns pointing up. It is not the moon, not a flame and not a comet: no fire, no smoke.

The lights are small glowing points painted in thick luminous brushstrokes, like stars in a Van Gogh night sky: a bright core with a soft ring of short curved paint dabs around it. Painted light, not a lens flare and not a CGI glow. The lights have no faces and no bodies.

Design sheet on a plain deep night-blue background: the four states drawn side by side in one row, to scale, with space between them.

Image 1 is the lights' design sheet: the amber light, first on the left, is the exact design, and its shape, colors and brushwork 100% match it. Only the amber light appears on this sheet. Image 2 is the little light's mood sheet, for the layout and the way a state is painted only; its pale blue color and round shape don't apply here.

No text, letters, labels, numbers, captions, watermark, signature or picture frame anywhere in the image. No photorealism, no 3D render, no anime, no flat vector illustration. No people, no figures and no faces anywhere. The lights have no faces. No other lights.
```

Check before approving:
- State 1 matches the amber light in CS1: the same crescent, horns up, the same amber and ring of dabs.
- All four keep the horns up; none turns into a moon, a circle or a flame.
- State 3 reads as a soft painted streak drifting down, with no fire, smoke or comet tail.
- Only the amber light: nothing pale blue copied from CS2, no faces, no text.

Hand-finish:
- Keep the core at the palette's Amber, with Amber glow around it.
- Paint your own amber dabs along the streak in state 3 so it matches the brushwork of the sky plates.

Attach: BG1 (the image to edit)

```
Edit the attached image: a night sky filled by one great swirl. Keep everything in it exactly the same except the change described below.

CHANGE: The same sky, colder, darker and lonelier. Most of the warm gold drains out of the two great bands of strokes and turns pale cold blue and blue-gray, leaving only a few faint gold touches; the cobalt deepens toward midnight blue.
```
