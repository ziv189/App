# tower: inside the Saint-Aube Clock Tower
Cold blue-grey stone interior, lit by a dim blue light falling from the ceiling above.
Far layer (0.2x): a wall of tall stone arches, pale blue light shafts slanting down between them, and one faded clock face stopped at 23:47.
Mid layer (0.45x): huge gear rings frozen mid-turn, with spokes, hubs and a cold highlight on the top rim.
Ropes sag from above and chains hang with brass weights, swaying only in a faint draft.
Stone walls are dark blue-grey blocks with thin black mortar lines; platforms are weathered grey-brown wooden beams with brass studs.
Spikes are brass; the lantern-clock is the only warm light and marks each save point.
The exit is an iron door with a 23:47 dial; gold rouages turn slowly, oil flasks glow amber, the arena grate has red-hot edges.
Foreground: slow dust motes drifting upward at 0.1 to 0.4 alpha, catching the blue light.
Palette: slate and navy stone, cold steel, aged wood, brass accents, one warm lantern.
