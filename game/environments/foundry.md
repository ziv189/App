# Foundry (theme `foundry`): the iron foundry under the Saint-Aube Tower
Furnaces that never went out, frozen steam, sparks hanging in the air.
Sky: a deep red-brown gradient with a dull orange glow low on the horizon.
Far: chimney stacks, some with a clock tower reading 23:47, and frozen steam banks that only drift with the camera.
Steam: pale puffs over the chimneys that never move, so they look caught mid-breath.
Mid: cooling pipes with frost on top, and arched furnace mouths spilling orange light.
Floor: orange glow bands and dark iron plates behind the tiles.
Tiles: dark riveted iron plates ('#') with glowing orange seams, rusty grates ('=') lit from below.
Props: rusty spikes with hot tips ('^'), lit lantern-clocks ('C'), a dial door ('X'), golden cogs ('o'), oil flasks ('h'), iron bars with hot edges ('B').
Foreground: slow orange embers rising at 0.1-0.4 alpha, swaying as they go.
