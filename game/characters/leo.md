# Leo Varin (`leo`)
- **Age:** 17 · **Role:** the player; apprentice clockmaker, son of Elias Varin
- **Logline:** Ten years after the Saint-Aube Tower stopped, the apprentice who let go of his sister's hand must climb the frozen Tower and choose: restart time, or keep the hour frozen.
## Look
- Midnight-blue knitted scarf with pale stripes, streaming behind him when he runs; brass goggles pushed up on the forehead; messy brown hair.
- Brown leather waistcoat over a cream shirt; brass pocket watch on a chain, ticking again from chapter 1.
- The Dawn Wrench: a huge brass wrench, swung in a wide arc with a visible wind-up.
- Palette: scarf #1f2c55 / #cdd8ea · brass #d4a94e · leather #6e4528 · shirt #f3e7cf · skin #f0c4a0 · hair #4a3020.
## Personality
- Anxious and quick-witted; sarcastic when scared, very gentle when he thinks of Juliette.
- Fears: that he caused the city to stop, that he will let go again, that he will become the Regent.
- Wants: to be forgiven, to give Juliette peace, and to stop feeling guilty.
## Voice
- "I'm fine. My hands are shaking, that's all. Totally normal."
- "Juliette... wait for me. Please, just one more minute."
- "Brave? No. I'm just the one who didn't run."
## Relationships
- Juliette (sister, died at 6): the source of his guilt; he blames himself for her fall.
- Elias (father): first blamed, then understood as a father who stopped the Tower to save a child.
- Mireille Crow: a thief who steals his watch and becomes a sharp, protective ally.
- Gaspard calls him "sir" and mourns Elias; the Regent is the mirror, a grief turned into a cage.
## Arc
- Ch1, Vermeil Station: arrives at 23:47; the watch ticks again; hears "Leo, you won't let go?"
- Ch2, The Frozen Market: Mireille steals the watch; learns about Tomas; unlocks the dash.
- Ch3, The Foundries: Gaspard says "Your father only wanted to gain one minute"; anger at Elias; unlocks the pendulum.
- Ch4, Inside the Tower: admits "I let go of your hand"; finds the spring (double jump); defeats the Regent.
- Ch5, The Dial: forgives himself and chooses "Restart the clock" (Time Resumes) or "Keep the hour frozen" (The Eternal Hour).
