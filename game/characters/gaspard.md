# Gaspard
- **Name:** Gaspard, a brass automaton built by Elias. **Age:** he gives "a respectable number of years" and never more.
- **Role:** Nanny and butler of the Varin house; asleep in the Foundry boiler room until Leo wakes him.
- **Logline:** A proud brass nanny who kept the fires burning for ten years, waiting for a family that never came back down the stairs, must decide whether he may finally rest.

## Look and palette
- Tall brass body in a dark-green tailcoat with tails, white collar, brass buttons; oval brass head, dark visor, one glowing cyan glass eye, one dim bronze dial, grille mouth of small slats.
- Gears turn in a window on his chest; steam puffs from the back of his neck; slow, heavy steps. Palette: brass #c9963f, coat #1f3d34, glass #6fd3ff.

## Personality, fears, wants
- Formal, polite, dry; emotional under the manners. Calls Leo "young man", then "Master Leo", then "Monsieur Leo".
- Fears failing the children and being left to run down alone. Wants the family whole again, and leave to rest.

## Voice
- "Ten years is a long nap, even for a machine of my dignity."
- "He stopped the Tower to win one minute, young man. One minute, to save your sister."
- "I kept the fires burning for ten years, waiting for him to come back down the stairs."

## Relationships
- Leo: raised him as a nanny does; sees the guilt he carries and will not let him carry it alone.
- Juliette: his charge. Elias: his maker; he forgave him long ago, unasked.
- Mireille and Tomas: finds Mireille quick but respects her; gentle with Tomas, the boy caught mid-laugh.

## Arc
- Ch1: Not seen. Asleep in the boiler room; the watch ticking in Leo's pocket is the first sound that will reach him.
- Ch2: Still absent. Hugo grumbles that Elias built the only automaton in Vermeil with manners.
- Ch3: Woken by the watch, he tells part of the night, sends Leo on, and stays behind to keep the fires.
- Ch4: Offstage, keeping the freight lift running from below and waiting to be needed.
- Ch5: At the summit he finds his dignity and admits he would like to rest. "Restart the clock": he bows, his glass eye stops blinking, and he is free. "Keep the hour frozen": he stays as keeper of the hour, a brass hand on Leo's shoulder.
