# The Regent (Aurelien Vantard)

**Age:** 58 (the mask makes him look older). **Role:** Final boss of chapter 4, "Inside the Tower", former partner of Élias Varin.
**Logline:** A grieving clockmaker who wants the city frozen forever so that nothing is ever lost again, and who has made the Saint-Aube Tower his fortress.

**Look:** Very tall (about 120 px), a black frock coat to the knees with a deep purple lining and gold gear buttons, white gloves, and a brass cane with a clock-face pommel. A porcelain clock mask covers the right half of his face, with gold ticks and hands stopped at 23:47. Phase 2 adds cracks to the mask. Phase 3 tears the coat and four gears orbit him.
**Palette:** coat #1a1a24, lining #3d1f4f, gold #d9b35a, porcelain #efe9dc, cracks #4a4048, silver hair #c9c4cc, skin #e6c3a5, gloves #f4f1ea, sand beam #ffd27a.

**Personality:** Poetic, cold, patient, certain he is right. Long measured sentences. Calls people "my boy" even when he means to crush them.
**Fears:** Losing anyone again. The sound of a clock running down. Having wasted Constance's last hours.
**Wants:** The hour to stay frozen so that no one ever leaves. Failing that, an admission from Élias that the stopped minute was worth what it cost.

**Voice:**
- "Time is a thief with good manners, my boy. I merely locked the door behind it."
- "Constance had a fever and a clock that would not wait. I swore no clock would ever run without me again."
- "Every step you take toward her is a second you stole from me. Stand still, and you will feel nothing at all."

**Relationships:**
- **Léo:** a mirror. Léo let go of Juliette's hand and blames himself. The Regent refuses to let go of anything. He sees in Léo the boy who could still choose to stop holding on.
- **Élias:** his former partner. Élias stopped the clock to save his daughter. The Regent believes the minute should have been kept, and resents Élias for letting it go.
- **Juliette:** never met by the Regent. The frozen childhood she is stuck in is exactly what he wants to keep for everyone.
- **Gaspard, Hugo, Pivert, Mireille, the frozen crowd:** not personally connected. To him they are proof that the plan works.

**Arc:**
- **Ch1 Vermeil Station:** not seen. The Tower is named only, as the fortress above the station.
- **Ch2 The Frozen Market:** Old Hugo mentions "Vantard", Élias's old partner, who "never let anything go".
- **Ch3 The Foundries:** Gaspard hints at a second man in the Tower that night, one Élias never mentions.
- **Ch4 Inside the Tower:** bars the arena door and fights in three phases. After his defeat he confesses that Constance died of fever and that time is a betrayal. He falls and the door opens.
- **Ch5 The Dial:** not present in person. His mask lies on the summit balustrade. Élias speaks of him as the man who wanted what Élias wanted, and nearly lost everything for it.
