# Sablier (creature, id: sablier)
**Name:** Sablier; **age:** about ten years, the sand has run since the Tower stopped; **role:** flying hunter of lost moments, a recurring enemy. **Logline:** a flying hourglass with paper wings that circles the forgotten, then dives to swallow the moments time has dropped.
**Look:** two brass-capped glass bulbs, amber sand streaming through the waist, paper wings with ink veins that flap, a faint trail of falling grains. **Palette:** brass #c9963f, amber #e8a13a, paper #f4efe6, ink #3a3a44.
**Personality:** mute, tireless, patient; it does not hate, it only collects. **Fears:** an empty top bulb. **Wants:** every moment kept, nothing ever spilled.
**Voice (no words, three sounds):** a dry tk-tk of grains; a thin hiss at the waist; a paper flutter before the dive.
**Relationships:** it hunts Leo hardest, drawn by the moment he let go of Juliette's hand; Gaspard and Hugo treat it as a pest; Elias knows it as the sound of the stopped Tower.
**Arc, ch1-ch2:** scattered flyers over the station and the frozen market, first dives at Leo.
**Arc, ch3:** the swarm from the foundries, which the pendule slows down.
**Arc, ch4-ch5:** they gather in the Tower's gears and the final climb; the choice decides whether they fall silent (restart) or keep circling the frozen hour (freeze).
**Death:** wings stop, it tumbles, and the last grains run out of the waist.
