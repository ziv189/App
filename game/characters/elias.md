# Elias Varin (`elias`)
- **Age:** 40 when the hour stopped, still 40 in the Tower. Grey-haired since that night.
- **Role:** Master clockmaker of the Saint-Aube Tower, Leo's and Juliette's father. Now half machine, holding the balance wheel.
- **Logline:** A father stopped the city's clock for one minute to save his daughter, and has held that minute for ten years.
## Look
- Tall and lean; long brown workshop coat with rolled sleeves; grey waistcoat with a watch chain; spectacles pushed down; neat grey hair.
- Left half of the face is brass: a cheek gear that turns, and a clock-dial eye reading 23:47. It is his own left, so it sits on the viewer's right in the portrait.
- Left arm partly brass, with visible shoulder, elbow and wrist joints.
- Palette: coat #6b4a2e, waistcoat #8f959d, brass #c9963f and #e6c26d, skin #e8c3a0, hair #aaa7a2, trousers #3b3d4a, dial #f6efe1.
## Personality
Tender, tired, precise. Speaks in clockwork metaphors and never raises his voice.
- **Fears:** that Leo carries the minute his whole life; that Juliette stays six forever.
- **Wants:** to set the balance wheel down, and to see Leo set down his guilt.
## Voice
- "A clock does not hurry, Leo. It only keeps its promises, one tick at a time."
- "I asked for one minute. I have held it for ten years, and my hands are tired."
- "Let go of the balance and the hour will run. That is not betrayal. It is how time is kept."
## Relationships
- **Leo:** still carries his watch; has never blamed him, not even for the hand he let go.
- **Juliette:** the reason for the stopped hour; she calls Leo "Léo-Grand".
- **Gaspard:** the nurse automaton he built, taught to care rather than obey.
- **The Regent:** former partner; they built the Tower's clock together, then split. Elias stayed inside it.
- **Others:** Hugo respects him; Mireille, Tomas, Pivert and Bastien are strangers he knows only as the stopped city.
## Arc
- **Ch1 Vermeil Station:** heard, not seen. His watch ticks again in Leo's hand.
- **Ch2 The Frozen Market:** Hugo points to a toy Elias mended, still ticking, as the way to him.
- **Ch3 The Foundries:** Gaspard gives the partial truth, "Your father wanted only one minute"; the pendulum method is Elias's design.
- **Ch4 Inside the Tower:** a recording of him in the gear cage: "You were seven. Stopping the Tower was my choice, and mine alone to answer for."
- **Ch5 The Dial:** face to face at the balance wheel. He releases Gaspard and leaves the choice to Leo. Restart frees him; freeze keeps him at the wheel.
