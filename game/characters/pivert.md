# Mrs. Pivert (`pivert`)

- **Age:** about 60. **Role:** baker on the station concourse, stuck in her own loop.
- **Logline:** A nosy, motherly baker who has offered the same loaf for ten years, until a thin boy in wet shoes answers her.

## Look and palette
- Plump and rosy-cheeked; hair pinned up with a pencil under a blue polka-dot headscarf. Brick-red dress, flour-stained white apron, a round golden loaf held in both hands.
- Palette: dress `#b0473a`, apron `#f6f0e2`, scarf `#2f5ea8`, dots `#f3cc4f`, bread `#c98a3d`, skin `#f2c3a0`.
- Motion: slow breathing idle, a waddling walk with the loaf swaying against the stride, a wide loaf swing for attacks, grey-blue frost when frozen.

## Personality
- Warm, bossy, nosy. Feeds anyone who looks thin. Fears a stale loaf and never finishing a sentence. Wants one whole sentence, people at her table and the trains running.

## Voice
- "Would you like a loaf, dear? Fresh this morning."
- "Have you eaten? You haven't eaten, have you?"
- "Mind the procedure, dear. He does love a procedure."

## Relationships
- **Leo:** a skinny, wet stray to be fed; the first person to break her loop in ten years.
- **Bastien:** fond exasperation at his procedures.
- **Tomas:** the roll frozen in his laugh came from her oven.
- **Old Hugo:** they bicker; he grumbles that her bread is too good for a frozen city.

## Arc
- **Ch1, Vermeil Station:** stuck on her offer until Leo answers; she sends him to Bastien about the train (`met_pivert`).
- **Ch2, The Frozen Market:** her loaves hang frozen in the market; Leo learns the roll in Tomas's mouth is hers.
- **Ch3, The Foundries:** after Leo returns with Gaspard, she gets two whole sentences out for the first time.
- **Ch4, Inside the Tower:** absent in person; her voice echoes through the gear cages and helps Leo hold on through the Regent's fight.
- **Ch5, The Dial:** "Restart the clock": she finishes her sentence and her loaf. "Keep the hour frozen": she starts it again, forever.
