# Tomas
- **Name:** Tomas. **Age:** 8. **Id:** `tomas`
- **Role:** Mireille's little brother, caught mid-laugh in the Frozen Market with a bread roll.
- **Logline:** The boy whose laugh was cut off in the middle, and whose only words are "Again!" and "Big sister!".
## Look
- Striped navy-and-cream jumper, short messy brown hair, wide open laughing mouth, rosy cheeks.
- One knee bent as if mid-run; the bread roll is held out in front of him.
- Frozen look: a slight grey stillness that drains away when he moves (walk, run, talk).
- Palette: navy `#2d3f6e`, cream `#f1e7d0`, skin `#f1c7a0`, hair `#8c5a2e`, cheeks `#ef8a82`, bread `#e6a95e`, trousers `#6b4a2a`, boots `#3a2a22`.
- Note: the ch2 text has him "frozen with a bread roll in his mouth"; the art holds it out in front. Both readings work.
## Personality
- Pure appetite and joy: bread, jokes, being swung around. Almost no words.
- Fears: losing his place in the joke. Mireille, ch5: "He'll be furious he lost his place in the joke."
- Wants: to finish the laugh, to have his big sister close, and one more go at the game.
## Voice (1 to 3 words; he is nearly mute)
- "Bread..."
- "Big sister!"
- "Again!"
## Relationships
- Mireille: his big sister and his reason. "Tomas is my reason."
- Leo: a stranger who becomes "Big brother!" in the freeze ending. Tomas's frozen laugh is what the stopped minute looks like on a face; "Again!" is the wish Leo's father never let finish.
- Madame Pivert: the bread thread. In the restart ending her unfinished loaf and his unbitten roll finish together.
## Arc
- Ch1 La Gare de Vermeil: off-stage, not mentioned yet.
- Ch2 Le Marché Figé: met frozen in the market. Mireille's "Tomas is my reason" is what turns Leo toward the Tower. His repeat lines ("Again!") make the stillness personal.
- Ch3 Les Fonderies: off-stage. Mireille's stake stays in the background while the furnaces take the spotlight.
- Ch4 Entrailles de la Tour: off-stage. The reason to climb stays in the player's mind.
- Ch5 Le Cadran: at the summit Mireille says he would be first up, yelling "Encore!", but he is still in the market. Restart the clock: he bursts out laughing, bites the roll, "Again!". Keep the hour frozen: he stays mid-laugh, roll halfway to his mouth, "Big brother!".
