# Mireille Crow

- **Age and role:** 22, thief and ally (French bible: Mireille Corbeau). **Logline:** a quick, sly thief who wants the clock restarted for her frozen brother, and ends up trusting Leo.

## Look and palette
- Bright red hair in two long braids; a lockpick tucked in her hair (twirled when idle); a short dagger for attacks.
- Short plum velvet jacket, brass buttons, fingerless black gloves. Lean and quick, a little smaller than Leo. Palette: hair #d2402c, jacket #5d2149, brass #d8a94c, gloves #1b1820.

## Personality, fears, wants
- Fast, cynical, funny, protective of anyone smaller than her. Fears being too late again: she was out on a job the night Tomas froze.
- Wants Tomas running in the street again, and a Tower that stops taking people's time.

## Voice
- "Your watch? Relax, I only borrowed it. Nobody misses what they never felt leave."
- "Ten seconds and a bent hairpin. A lock is just a question nobody bothered to answer."
- "Tomas, one more crumb on that floor and I'm telling Mrs. Pivert."

## Relationships
- **Leo:** starts as a mark, becomes a partner. She recognises the guilt she sees in him.
- **Tomas:** her little brother, frozen mid-laugh with a bread roll. He calls her "Big sis!"
- **Gaspard:** teases him as "Tin Butler"; he is the one who makes her polite.
- **Elias, the Regent:** admires Elias from afar; knows the Regent's collection too well.
- **Hugo, Mrs. Pivert, Bastien:** regulars she robs, charms or ignores.

## Arc, chapters 1 to 5
- **Ch1:** off-screen. Optional flavour: a wanted notice for "the red-haired thief" in the station office.
- **Ch2, The Frozen Market:** lifts Leo's watch, then trades it back for his help with the clock. Teaches him the dash, "la glisse de Mireille".
- **Ch3, The Foundries:** joins the trip, covers Leo against the sandglasses, and admits what happened to Tomas.
- **Ch4:** not in the DESIGN scene list. Suggestion: she waits outside the arena door and talks Leo through the fight.
- **Ch5, The Dial:** reunites with Tomas at the summit and backs Leo's choice. Restart: she runs down the stairs with Tomas. Freeze: she stays and keeps the frozen city fed.
