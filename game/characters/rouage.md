# Rouage (creature)
- Where it lives: the gear cages and drains under Vermeil station, the foundry floor, and the Tower's gear shafts.
- Look: round brass gear body, red eye, two pincers, four spring legs, coiled antennae. Brass #c8963c, dark brass #7d5219, eye #d9372b.
- Behaviour: skitters on its spring legs, fast and jumpy; turns at walls; snaps its pincers when Leo gets close.
- Attack: winds up, lunges with the pincers wide open, then recovers. Fragile: two hits and it breaks.
- Death: its gears fly apart, land, spin down and fade.
- Story: a scrap of the Tower's wheelwork that broke loose the night the clock stopped. Everything else in Vermeil froze; these still move.
- Chapter 1: the first fight, on the platform among the frozen crowd. It teaches the attack key (X or J).
- Chapters 3 and 4: a pack in the foundry steam, then more in the gear cages, waiting between the turning wheels.
- Arc: none. It is a hazard, not a character: no dialogue, no portrait.
